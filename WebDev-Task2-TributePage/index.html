Her Story

Jessica McCabe is an American YouTuber, writer, and educator best known for creating the educational YouTube channel How to ADHD. Through her work, she helps people understand attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD).

Jessica has shared her own experiences with ADHD and the challenges she faced while trying to understand how her brain works. These experiences inspired her to learn more about ADHD and share useful information with others.

Her YouTube channel explains ADHD-related topics through accessible videos, personal stories, humor, and practical strategies. Her goal is to help people feel understood and discover approaches that work for their everyday lives.

Jessica is also an author. Her book, How to ADHD: An Insider's Guide to Working with Your Brain (Not Against It), extends her work beyond YouTube. Her efforts encourage understanding, self-acceptance, and better support for people with ADHD.
